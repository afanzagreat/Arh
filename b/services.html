04 / Before you start

Good questions.
Straight answers.

Still figuring it out? That is a perfectly good place to start a conversation.

How long does a website take?

Most sites launch in 2–4 weeks after the content and design direction are approved. Timing depends on the project and how quickly the needed material and feedback are ready.

Can you redesign my current site?

Yes. We can look at an existing site and discuss whether it needs an audit, repairs, or a more complete redesign.

Do I need a monthly plan?

No. A one-time build is available too. Ongoing support and renewal terms are explained before you commit.

What if I’m not sure what I need?

Start with what your business does and what you want the website to help people do. We can work out a sensible starting point together.

Do you work with small local businesses?

Absolutely. ARH is built around independent owners and small local teams, based in Jeddah and working beyond it.
