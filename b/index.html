05 / Before you start

Good questions.
Straight answers.

Still figuring it out? That is a perfectly good place to start a conversation.

How long does a website take?

Most sites launch in 2–4 weeks after the content and design direction are approved.

Can you redesign my current site?

Yes. We can audit, repair, or redesign an existing site depending on what it needs.

Do I need a monthly plan?

No. A one-time build is available too. Ongoing support and renewal terms are explained before you commit.

What if I’m not sure what I need?

Tell us what your business does and what you would like the website to help with. We can work out a sensible starting point together.

Do you work with small local businesses?

Absolutely. ARH is built around independent owners and small local teams, based in Jeddah and working beyond it.
