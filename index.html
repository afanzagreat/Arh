06 — FROM THE STUDIO

THOUGHTS ON
WHAT WORKS.

Short, practical notes on what actually helps a local business website convert visitors into customers.

HOSPITALITY

Why your restaurant needs online booking

A phone line only works when someone's free to answer it. Here's what a booking button actually recovers.

Read more +

Every unanswered call during a dinner rush is a booking that goes to the next restaurant on the list. A simple "Book a table" button on your homepage works 24/7 — no missed calls, no back-and-forth. Even a basic form that emails you a request cuts that loss dramatically, and it costs nothing to run once it's built.

LOCAL SEO

Why "just being on Google Maps" isn't enough

Showing up in a map search is step one. Here's what actually gets the click after that.

Read more +

A Google Business listing gets you found — but people still click through to check if a business looks legitimate before calling or visiting. A basic website with real photos, clear hours, and a way to contact you turns that click into a customer instead of a bounce back to the search results.

TRUST

The 5-second trust test

Visitors decide whether to trust a small business site almost instantly. Here's what they're actually judging.

Read more +

In the first few seconds, people are unconsciously checking: does this look current, does it load fast, is there a real way to contact someone. An outdated template, a dead contact form, or a site that looks abandoned quietly costs you customers before they ever read your actual offer.

How long does a website take to build? +

Most sites launch in 2–4 weeks once we have your content and sign-off on the design direction.

Can you redesign my existing site? +

Yes — we can audit, repair, or fully redesign an existing website depending on what it actually needs.

Do I have to commit to a monthly plan? +

No. You can pay for a one-off build. Monthly plans just keep your site secure, updated and improving over time.

Do you work with small or one-person businesses? +
